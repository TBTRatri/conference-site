Legacy of Service Since 1981

Leading the Vanguard of Humanitarian Medical Service

Medicine Club Bangladesh is a premier non-political voluntary organization established by medical and dental students. For over four decades, our units spanning medical colleges nationwide have been running continuous voluntary blood donation drives, free health clinics, and disaster relief.

This year's Central Annual Conference gathers delegates, chapter presidents, alumni physicians, and visionary leaders to deliberate future strategies and empower the next generation of doctors.
