How to Reach Savar

Venue Transit Guide

PHA Bhaban, Gonoshasthaya Kendra, Mirzanagar, Savar, Dhaka

From Gabtoli / Dhaka City

Take any direct bus towards Nabinagar / Baipail (Thikana, Boishakhi, or Savar Paribahan). Get down at Mirzanagar Bus Stand, then take an auto-rickshaw directly to PHA Bhaban.

From Airport / Uttara

Board the Ashulia / Baipail highway bus or direct ride-share via Abdullahpur - Baipail bypass road to Mirzanagar / Gonoshasthaya Kendra gate.

Parking & Security

Designated vehicle parking and volunteer reception desks are operational at the main PHA Bhaban compound starting Wednesday 4:00 PM.
